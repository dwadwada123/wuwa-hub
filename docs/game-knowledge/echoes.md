# Wuthering Waves — Version 3.7 Echo & Sonata Knowledge Base

Echoes form the gear and artifact foundation in Wuthering Waves. They provide main stats, substats, active Echo skills, and Sonata Set bonuses.

---

## 1. Sonata Sets (Updated to Version 3.7)

### 1. Heart of Sworn Vigil (Introduced in 3.7)
- **2-Piece**: Electro DMG +10%
- **5-Piece**: Inflicting Electro Flare on the target, obtaining Unison, or triggering Unison Response increases Crit. Rate by 15% and grants 22.5% Electro DMG for 30s.

### 2. Moonlit Clouds (Premier Sub-DPS & Buffer Set)
- **2-Piece**: Energy Regen +10%
- **5-Piece**: After using an Outro Skill, the ATK of the next Resonator to enter the field is increased by 22.5% for 15 seconds.
- **Recommended Users**: Yinlin, Zhezhi, Sanhua, Mortefi, Yangyang, Aalto, Roccia.
- **Primary 4-Cost Echo**: Impermanence Heron (+12% DMG buff to next resonator on active skill hit).

### 3. Rejuvenating Glow (Premier Healer Set)
- **2-Piece**: Healing Bonus +10%
- **5-Piece**: When healing allies, the ATK of all party members is increased by 15% for 30 seconds.
- **Recommended Users**: Shorekeeper, Verina, Baizhi, Youhu, Buling.
- **Primary 4-Cost Echo**:
  - Shorekeeper: Fallacy of No Return (+10% Energy Regen & +10% ATK).
  - Verina / Baizhi: Bell-Borne Geochelone (50% DMG reduction shield + 10% DMG amp).

## 2. Echo Cost Distributions & Stat Prioritization

### Optimal Cost Distribution: 4-3-3-1-1 (Total Cost: 12)
- **4-Cost**: Crit Rate / Crit DMG (for DPS) or Healing Bonus (for Sustain).
- **3-Cost (x2)**: Elemental DMG% corresponding to character element (or Energy Regen% for supports like Shorekeeper/Verina).
- **1-Cost (x2)**: ATK% (or HP% for Shorekeeper/Baizhi, DEF% for Yuanwu/Taoqi).

### Substat Priority (DPS)
`Crit Rate = Crit DMG > Elemental DMG% / ATK% > Resonance Liberation DMG% / Skill DMG% > Energy Regen%`
