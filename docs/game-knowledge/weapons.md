## 2. Standard 5-Star Weapons

- **Emerald of Genesis** (Sword, Crit Rate): Premier generalist 5★ sword. Energy Regen + ATK stacking on Skill.
- **Lustrous Razor** (Broadblade, ATK%): Energy Regen + Resonance Liberation DMG on Skill cast.
- **Abyss Surges** (Gauntlets, Energy Regen): Increases ATK; Basic and Skill hits boost each other.
- **Cosmic Ripples** (Rectifier, Energy Regen): Basic attacks boost Basic ATK DMG.
- **Static Mist** (Pistols, Crit Rate): Casting Outro Skill increases incoming teammate's ATK by 10%.

---

## 3. High-Value 4-Star Options

- **Variation** (Rectifier, Energy Regen): Casting Resonance Skill restores 8 Concerto Energy (Cooldown 20s). Best-in-slot 4★ for Verina, Shorekeeper, Baizhi.
- **Discord** (Broadblade, Energy Regen): Restores Concerto Energy on Skill cast. Great for Taoqi and support builds.
- **Autumntrace** (Broadblade, Crit Rate): Pioneer Podcast battle-pass weapon; stacks ATK on basic/heavy hits. Great budget Jiyan/Calcharo weapon.
- **Originite: Type IV** (Gauntlets, DEF%): Normal attacks trigger healing, enabling the wielder (e.g., Yuanwu) to activate the 5-pc Rejuvenating Glow team ATK buff effortlessly!
