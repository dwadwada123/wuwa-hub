## 1. Version Overview & Core Mechanics

Version 3.7 introduces the **Mengzhou Sentinel** storyline arc alongside a revolutionary combat subsystem: **The Unison Mechanic**.

### The Unison Mechanic
- **Unison Points & State**: Resonators equipped with Unison passives (such as Hsin and Suoming) accumulate Unison through specialized attack sequences and Concerto consumption.
- **Unison Response**: Allows an off-field Resonator to enter the field via an instant **Unison Intro** without fully discharging the 100-point Concerto meter, enabling hyper-fluid quickswap combos.
- **Electro Flare Synergy**: Status effect applied to enemies that amplifies sustained Electro damage and triggers secondary coordinated lightning pulses.

## 4. Meta & Team Building Impact
1. **Electro Supremacy**: The introduction of Hsin + Heart of Sworn Vigil significantly elevates Electro team archetypes, enabling high-synergy dual-Electro cores (Hsin + Xiangli Yao, Hsin + Yinlin, Hsin + Suoming).
2. **Concerto Efficiency**: Unison allows teams to rotate faster than traditional 20-second cycles, compressing rotation loops into 14-16 seconds.
3. **Shorekeeper & Verina Longevity**: Universal supports remain Tier 0, with Shorekeeper providing unmatched Crit/Crit DMG coverage and Verina maintaining unrivaled universal Deepen and fast rotation uptime.
